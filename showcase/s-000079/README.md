# S79 — Ten Procedural Creatures

A 20-second procedural-creature reel built entirely from typed MotionLoom
primitive and compound assets. Ten small characters share materials, eyes,
staged lighting and a common gallery floor while their silhouettes and motions
remain deliberately distinct.

The example demonstrates reusable asset composition, deterministic keyframe
animation, toon-friendly staging and camera cuts without a GLB, external image,
or pre-rendered character. `main.motionloom` is the canonical source.
